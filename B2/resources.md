## Weekly B2 loop (sample)

1. Aspekte/Sicher B2 unit: text → outline → 120-word summary  
2. One DW B2 article: listen without text, then read, then shadow  
3. Easy German Podcast or Lage der Nation (20–30 min) with 5 new collocations  
4. Stilistik/Textstrukturen drill (connectors, Nominalstil rewrite)  
5. Formal email or discussion paragraph to the B2 templates in-repo
