# A1 Study Resources

Level-matched materials only. Prefer named units over generic “watch YouTube”.

## Coursebooks (pick one spine)

| Book | Publisher | Use at A1 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Netzwerk neu A1** (Kursbuch + Arbeitsbuch) | Hueber | Primary classroom path; units 1–12 map to A1 topics |
| **Menschen A1** (Kursbuch + Arbeitsbuch) | Hueber | Strong dialogues and phonetics |
| **Grammatik aktiv A1–B1** (Hueber) | Hueber | Parallel grammar drills; stay in A1 chapters only |

Do **not** start Aspekte/Sicher at A1.

## Deutsche Welle (free, structured)

| Resource | Level fit | URL | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|-----|
| **Nicos Weg** (A1 track) | A1 | https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789 | Video course with grammar pages; complete A1 arc |
| **Deutschtrainer** | A1 | https://learngerman.dw.com/en/deutschtrainer/c-56705009 | 100 everyday topics, bilingual audio, pronunciation |
| **Artikeltrainer** | A1 | https://learngerman.dw.com/de/artikeltrainer | der/die/das under time pressure |
| **Beginners hub** | A1–A2 | https://learngerman.dw.com/en/beginners/s-62078399 | Entry index for all beginner courses |
| **DW Learn German home** | all | https://learngerman.dw.com/en/learn-german/s-9528 | Placement and course catalog |

**Suggested order:** Deutschtrainer (vocabulary) → Nicos Weg A1 episodes in sequence → Artikeltrainer 10 min daily.

## Weekly A1 loop (sample)

1. One **Nicos Weg** A1 episode + DW grammar page  
2. 20 **Deutschtrainer** vocab items → Anki  
3. One **Grammatik aktiv** A1 worksheet  
4. One **Super Easy German** video, German subtitles only, then with English  
5. Weekend: **Coffee Break German** recap + Artikeltrainer
