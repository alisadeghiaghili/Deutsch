## Weekly B1 loop (sample)

1. Aspekte/Netzwerk B1 unit (text + exercises)  
2. DW “Profis gesucht” or B1 article: listen → summarize 5 sentences in German  
3. Easy German Intermediate interview: shadow 30 seconds  
4. Grammar: one Konjunktiv or Passiv worksheet  
5. Easy German Podcast episode at 1.0×, then 0.85× for hard stretches
