## Special Characters

### Umlauts (Umlaute)

| Letter | Pronunciation | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| **ä** | like "e" in "bed" or "æ" | Mädchen (دختر) |
| **ö** | like "eu" in French "peu" (rounded "ö") | Schöffel (قاشق) |
| **ü** | like French "u" (rounded "ü") | über (بالای) |

**How to pronounce ö:**
1. Say "ee" as in "see"
2. Round your lips as if saying "oo"
3. The sound between "ee" and "oo" is ö

**How to pronounce ü:**
1. Say "ee" as in "see"
2. Round your lips tightly
3. The sound is ü

### Eszett (ß)

| Letter | Name | Pronunciation | Example |
|--------|------|---------------|---------|
| **ß** | Eszett / sharp s | like "ss" in "glass" | Straße (خیابان) |

## Vowel Sounds

| Vowel | Sound | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **a** | "ah" as in "father" | Vater (پدر) |
| **e** | "eh" as in "bed" | Bett (تخت) |
| **i** | "ee" as in "see" | Kindle (بچه) |
| **o** | "oh" as in "go" | Sohn (پسر) |
| **u** | "oo" as in "moon" | Buch (کتاب) |

## Diphthongs

| Diphthong | Sound | Example |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **ei/ai** | like "eye" in "eye" | Eins (یک), MAINZ |
| **au** | like "ow" in "cow" | Auto (ماشین) |
| **eu/äu** | like "oy" in "boy" | neu (جدید), Häuser (خانه‌ها) |

## Consonant Clusters

| Cluster | Sound | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **sch** | like "sh" in "shoe" | Schule (مدرسه) |
| **ch** | soft "h" (after e/i) or harsh (after a/o/u) | ich (من), Buch (کتاب) |
| **pf** | like "pf" in "pfeffer" | Pferd (اسب) |
| **tz** | like "ts" in "cats" | Platz (میدان) |
| **sp/st** | like "shp/sht" at beginning | Sport (ورزش), Straße (خیابان) |

## Common Pronunciation Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct | Explanation |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Pronouncing "w" as "w" in English | "v" sound | Wasser = "Vasser" |
| Pronouncing "v" as "v" in English | "f" sound | Vater = "Fater" |
| Pronouncing "j" as "j" in English | "y" sound | Ja = "Ya" |
| Pronouncing "z" as "z" in English | "ts" sound | Zeit = "Tsait" |
| Not rolling "r" | Guttural "r" | Practice with "Rrr" |
| Ignoring ummlauts | Must pronounce | ä, ö, ü are different letters |
