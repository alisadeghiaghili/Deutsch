## 📖 Study Tips for B2

1. **Context is key**: B2 words are often used in formal/academic contexts
2. **Compound words**: Many B2 words are compounds (e.g., Klimawandel, Menschenrecht)
3. **Suffix patterns**: -keit (Nachhaltigkeit), -ung (Digitalisierung), -heit (Menschenwürde)
4. **Greek/Latin roots**: Many academic words come from Greek/Latin (Hypothese, Philosophie)
5. **Word families**: Learn related words together (analyieren, Analyse, Analytiker)
6. **Register awareness**: B2 words are often more formal than A1-B1 equivalents
7. **Practice reading**: B2 words appear in newspapers, academic texts, formal speeches
8. **Use in context**: Try to use each word in a sentence at least 3 times
