## Markdown Flashcards

Use these for studying in any text editor. Cover the right side and try to recall the answer.

### Menschen (People)

#### Front (Test Yourself)
**der ______** (man)
**die ______** (woman)
**das ______** (child)
**die ______** (boy)
**die ______** (teacher - male)
**die ______** (teacher - female)
**die ______** (mother)
**die ______** (father)

#### Back (Answer)
- **der Mann** = مرد (man) | Plural: die Männer | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **die Frau** = زن (woman) | Plural: die Frauen | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **das Kind** = کودک (child) | Plural: die Kinder | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **der Junge** = پسر (boy) | Plural: die Jungen | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **der Lehrer** = معلم مرد (male teacher) | Plural: die Lehrer | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **die Lehrerin** = معلم زن (female teacher) | Plural: die Lehrerinnen | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **die Mutter** = مادر (mother) | Plural: die Mütter | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]
- **der Vater** = پدر (father) | Plural: die Väter | Cases: [Nom] [Akk] [Dat] [Gen]

### Familie (Family)

#### Front
**die ______** (sister)
**der ______** (brother)
**die ______** (grandmother)
**der ______** (grandfather)
**die ______** (daughter)
**der ______** (son)
**die ______** (aunt)
**der ______** (uncle)

#### Back
- **die Schwester** = خواهر (sister) | Plural: die Schwestern
- **der Bruder** = برادر (brother) | Plural: die Brüder
- **die Großmutter** = مادربزرگ (grandmother) | Plural: die Großmütter
- **der Großvater** = پدربزرگ (grandfather) | Plural: die Großväter
- **die Tochter** = دختر (daughter) | Plural: die Töchter
- **der Sohn** = پسر (son) | Plural: die Söhne
- **die Tante** = عمه/خاله (aunt) | Plural: die Tanten
- **der Onkel** = عمو/دایی (uncle) | Plural: die Onkel

### Essen und Trinken (Food & Drink)

#### Front
**das ______** (bread)
**der ______** (coffee)
**die ______** (milk)
**das ______** (water)
**der ______** (apple)
**die ______** (banana)
**das ______** (egg)
**der ______** (cheese)

#### Back
- **das Brot** = نان (bread) | Plural: die Brote
- **der Kaffee** = قهوه (coffee) | Plural: die Kaffees
- **die Milch** = شیر (milk) | No plural
- **das Wasser** = آب (water) | No plural
- **der Apfel** = سیب (apple) | Plural: die Äpfel
- **die Banane** = موز (banana) | Plural: die Bananen
- **das Ei** = تخم مرغ (egg) | Plural: die Eier
- **der Käse** = پنیر (cheese) | No plural
