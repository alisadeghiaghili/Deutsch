## Usage Notes / Verwendungshinweise

### Formal vs. Informal / Formell vs. Informell

**Use formal (Sie) when:**
- Writing to strangers or superiors
- In business situations
- At the doctor's office
- At university with professors

**Use informal (du) when:**
- Talking to friends and family
- With children
- In casual settings
- With close colleagues

### Tips for Using These Phrases / Tipps für die Verwendung

1. **Practice regularly** - Use one phrase per day in real conversations
2. **Context matters** - Choose the right phrase for the situation
3. **Be natural** - Don't memorize phrases word-for-word
4. **Listen actively** - Pay attention to how native speakers respond
5. **Make mistakes** - It's okay to make mistakes while learning
6. **Ask for feedback** - Native speakers can help you improve
