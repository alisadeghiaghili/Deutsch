## Weekly A2 loop (sample)

1. Nicos Weg A2 + associated grammar  
2. Kurz und leicht: listen → re-listen → 5-word Anki export  
3. Grammatik aktiv A2 worksheet (one tense/connector)  
4. One Easy German interview (German subs only, then both)  
5. Coffee Break German episode + shadowing 3 sentences
