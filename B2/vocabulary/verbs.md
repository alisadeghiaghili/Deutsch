## 📖 Study Tips for B2 Verbs

1. **Learn the Perfekt form first**: German uses Perfekt more than Präteritum in spoken language
2. **Separable verbs**: Practice with the prefix (ein-, aus-, an-, auf-, etc.)
3. **Reflexive verbs**: Always use the reflexive pronoun (mich, dich, sich, etc.)
4. **Irregular patterns**: Many B2 verbs follow patterns (e.g., ei → ie → ie)
5. **Word families**: Learn related words (analysieren → Analyse → Analytiker)
6. **Context practice**: Use each verb in a full sentence
7. **Phrasal verbs**: German uses many compound verbs (nachdenken, vorbeugen)
8. **Formal register**: B2 verbs are often more formal than A1-B1 equivalents
